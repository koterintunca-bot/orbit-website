Step 1

Make sure your iPhone has them

Orbit shows the calendars your iPhone already has. Missing one, like your work calendar? Add that account on your iPhone:

Settings → Apps → Calendar → Calendar Accounts → Add Account

On iOS 17: Settings → Calendar → Accounts.

One calm view of your day

Your meetings sit next to your Orbit plans on Today and in the Calendar, and Orbit can remind you before them. Orbit only reads: nothing in your calendars is changed.

Your calendars stay on your iPhone

  • Read on your phone. Orbit never uploads your calendars, never saves them on a server and never puts them in your backup.
  • Busy only means just that. Orbit sees “busy 09:00–10:30”. What the meeting is, where and the notes never leave your phone.
  • Never changed. Orbit doesn’t add, move or delete anything in them.

The small print: when you ask Orbit’s AI something, or use Leave on time, the plans needed for that one answer go along for that moment and are not kept. Busy-only meetings only ever go as “Busy”.
