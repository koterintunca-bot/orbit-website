Questions

Good to know

Is Orbit only for ADHD or autistic people?

No. It was designed with ADHD and autistic people in mind, which makes it easy for anyone who wants a calmer, tidier day.

Do I have to talk to it?

No. You can type everything too. Talking is just faster when your head is full.

What happens if I don't finish things?

Nothing bad. They move to the next day with a gentle “no rush”. You can also tap “Make it lighter” when a day gets away from you.

Is my diary private?

Yes. Your voice is turned into text on your phone, only small relevant bits go to Orbit's AI, and backups are locked with your own password.

Does it work with my calendar?

Yes. Orbit can show the calendars already on your iPhone, your work calendar too, and it syncs with Google Calendar. Work meetings can stay private as “Busy”.

Can I share plans with my partner?

Yes. Couple mode lets you share plans with one person.

Which languages does it speak?

English, Spanish, Turkish, German, Italian and Portuguese.

Is there an Android version?

Not yet. Orbit is on iPhone (iOS 17 or later) first. Android is planned.

Is Orbit a medical or therapy app?

No. Orbit is a planner and diary. It doesn't diagnose or treat anything.

How do I cancel?

On your iPhone: Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Orbit. You won't be charged after the period you've paid for.
